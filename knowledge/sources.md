## Provenance and usage
The original PDFs and manufacturer images are not included in this repository. Keep separately in a private authorised working directory. Product photos, specifications and diagrams remain property of their rights holders. Do not republish manufacturer imagery as original CRAZYWATER material.

This register documents sources; it is **not** a full-text or image embedding index.
