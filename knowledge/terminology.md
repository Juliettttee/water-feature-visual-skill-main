# Bilingual water-feature terminology (working glossary)

| Chinese usage | Manufacturer / English anchor | Distinction |
|---|---|---|
| 跳泉 | OASE JumpingJet Rainbow Star / Rainbow Flash; Fontana Jumping Jets | Defined laminar water arc; do not substitute diffuse spray. |
| 层流喷泉 | Laminar jet | Laminar flow alone does not imply a cutting mechanism. |
| 拉线水帘 / 线帘 | OASE Mylar lace nozzle 22 MS is one example | Independent laces/strings; **not** woven steel mesh. |
| 钢网水幕 | Fine woven stainless-steel mesh water curtain (working descriptive label) | Fine mesh plane with water along surface. Exact proprietary product not verified in supplied catalogues. |
| 投影水幕 | Water screen / projection screen (generic) | Verify whether free-falling sheet, mist screen, or other physical construction. |
| 水膜瀑布 | Fontana Water-Film Effects | Not necessarily supported by cables or mesh. |
| 玻璃 / 镜面流水 | Fontana Glass/Mirror Waterfalls | Water moving across a supporting surface. |

## Verification rule
Product names are manufacturer labels, not automatically standard industry translations. User-approved Chinese names can override provisional labels without altering the technical product identification.

## Source locator notes
- OASE catalogue: JumpingJet family and Mylar-lace examples; confirm exact PDF pages against the source copy.
- Fontana catalogue: Jumping Jets, Laminar Jets, Water-Film Effects and Glass/Mirror Waterfalls in the contents.
- Crystal catalogue: visually inspect project pages; do not infer construction from photographs alone.
