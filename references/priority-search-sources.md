## Retrieval order / 检索顺序

1. Search the local catalogues for terminology and technical grounding, using OASE → Fontana → Crystal priority.
2. Search the priority sources above with the identified English term, Chinese aliases, project context and visual attributes.
3. Expand to Chinese platforms, manufacturer sites, architecture/landscape publications and general indexed sources.
4. Rank results by technical match, visual relevance and source credibility—not merely by company fame.
5. Mark each result as opened and visually inspected, indexed snippet only, authentication required, inaccessible, or historical/disabled.

These companies are a preferred discovery set, not an exclusive whitelist. Do not pad results with irrelevant projects and do not treat marketing images as verified engineering compliance.
